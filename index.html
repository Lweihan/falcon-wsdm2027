Abstract

User Interface Design (UI Design) refers to the process of designing human-computer interaction interfaces. This paper thoroughly explores the importance of UI design in modern society. We propose a novel approach that combines the advantages of grid layout and fluid layout to effectively improve user reading efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves significant improvements in both learnability and aesthetic quality.

Method

We adopt a modular design philosophy. Data is first cleaned through a preprocessing module, then fed into the core network. Our design follows the principles of "clarity" and "consistency" to ensure visual uniformity across all elements.
